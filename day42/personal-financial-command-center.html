Tips, Checklists & Resources

Freelancer Financial Checklist

    Core Tips

    • 💡 Save your tax % (~20-30%) the moment income lands, into a separate account.
    • 💡 Treat SIP like a fixed bill — automate it right after a payment clears.
    • 💡 Build emergency fund in a liquid fund, not just savings account.
    • 💡 Track income by client to know which relationships are worth protecting.
    • 💡 Raise rates gradually as your portfolio grows — don't undercharge forever.
