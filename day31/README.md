# 🚀 AI Supply Chain Control Tower

An interactive, AI-inspired Supply Chain Operations Simulation built using **HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript**. Step into the role of **Head of Operations** and make real-time strategic decisions to manage supply chain disruptions while maintaining critical business KPIs.

---

## 🌟 Overview

The **AI Supply Chain Control Tower** simulates real-world logistics and supply chain challenges in a fast-paced, decision-driven environment. Players receive live operational alerts, evaluate business impacts, and choose the best response before time runs out. Every decision affects operational performance, business KPIs, and the final performance score.

## 🚨 Operational Scenarios

The simulator randomly generates realistic business events, including:

- 🚢 Port Congestion
- 🏭 Supplier Delay
- 🚛 Truck Breakdown
- 📦 Warehouse Stockout
- 🛂 Customs Inspection
- 📈 Demand Spike
- ⚙️ Machine Failure
- 🌪 Weather Disruption
- 🔢 Inventory Mismatch
- 📉 Damaged Shipment

Each alert offers multiple strategic responses with different business outcomes.

## 🎯 Gameplay

1. Launch the simulation.
2. Analyze incoming operational alerts.
3. Select the best response before the timer expires.
4. Improve KPIs and maximize your score.
5. Complete the simulation to receive your final performance grade.
