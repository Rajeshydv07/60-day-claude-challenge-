# 🚀 LinkedIn Roast & Rebuild

**LinkedIn Roast & Rebuild** is an AI-powered web application that analyzes LinkedIn profiles, provides honest recruiter-style feedback, rewrites key profile sections, and generates a personalized 7-day optimization plan. It helps students, professionals, freelancers, and job seekers create a stronger LinkedIn presence that attracts recruiters and career opportunities.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔥 AI Profile Roast
- Recruiter-style first impression analysis
- Reviews headline, About section, experience, and skills
- Identifies weak points and missed opportunities
- Explains why each issue affects profile visibility

### 🛠️ AI Profile Rebuild
- Generates **3 optimized LinkedIn headline options**
- Rewrites the About section
- Improves experience bullet points
- Suggests better skills and keywords
- Optimizes content for recruiters and LinkedIn search

### 📊 Before vs After Scorecard
- Section-wise score comparison
- Overall profile strength score
- Improvement tracking

### 📅 Personalized 7-Day Growth Plan
- Daily profile improvement checklist
- LinkedIn content ideas
- Networking strategy
- Connection request template
- Engagement roadmap

### 📋 Summary Report
- Biggest profile mistakes
- Overall improvement score
- One-click copyable summary

# ⚙️ How It Works

1. Choose whether you already have a LinkedIn profile.
2. Enter your LinkedIn details.
3. Submit the form.
4. Claude AI analyzes your profile.
5. Receive:
   - Honest recruiter-style roast
   - Optimized profile rewrite
   - Better headline suggestions
   - Skills recommendations
   - Before vs After score comparison
   - 7-Day LinkedIn growth plan
