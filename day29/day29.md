# 🚀 Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## 📖 Project Overview

**Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab** is an interactive web-based educational simulation designed to provide practical experience in **Supply Chain Management, Crisis Response, Procurement, Logistics, Executive Leadership, and Artificial Intelligence (AI).**

The application places users in the role of a **Supply Chain Executive** responsible for managing a company during unexpected business disruptions. Every strategic decision directly impacts business performance, customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, profitability, and organizational resilience.

Unlike traditional learning methods, this simulator allows users to experience realistic crisis scenarios through interactive decision-making, making it an ideal learning platform for students, educators, and professionals.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🏢 1. Dynamic Company Generation

Every simulation begins with a randomly generated company profile containing:

- 🏭 Company Name
- 🏷️ Industry Type
- 💰 Annual Revenue
- 👥 Employees
- 🏗️ Manufacturing Facilities
- 📦 Warehouses
- 🤝 Supplier Network
- 📅 Inventory Availability
- 🚚 Lead Time
- 🌍 Operating Countries

Each simulation is unique, providing a different learning experience.

---

## 🚨 2. Intelligent Crisis Simulation

Randomly generated business disruptions include:

- 🔥 Factory Fire
- 💸 Supplier Bankruptcy
- ⚓ Global Port Strike
- 🛡️ Ransomware Attack
- 🌊 Flood Disaster
- 🪨 Raw Material Shortage
- 🌐 Geopolitical Trade Conflict
- 🚢 Global Shipping Delay

Each crisis provides:

- 📖 Business Description
- 📉 Business Impact
- ⚠️ Risk Severity
- 💡 Expert Recommendations
- 🔍 Vulnerability Assessment

---

## ⚔️ 3. Crisis War Room

Users must select **three strategic response actions** from multiple business options.

Available strategies include:

- 🔗 Emergency Backup Suppliers
- ✈️ Air Freight
- 📦 Inventory Reallocation
- 💰 Demand Shaping
- 📣 Customer Communication
- ⚙️ Lean Operations

Every decision affects:

- 💵 Cost
- 📦 Inventory
- 📈 Profit
- 🚚 Delivery Performance
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction

## 📊 4. Business Performance Dashboard

After selecting response strategies, users receive real-time business analytics.

Performance indicators include:

- 💰 Cost Efficiency

- 📈 Profitability
- 🚚 Delivery Speed
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction

Interactive charts and progress bars visualize decision outcomes instantly.
