# AI Interview Coach - Business Strategy Report

## Executive Summary

AI Interview Coach is an AI-powered mock interview platform designed for Indian students and job seekers. It provides personalized interview practice, answer scoring, skill-gap analysis, and company-specific preparation.

### Target Market

* Final-year B.Tech, BCA, MBA students
* Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges
* Training & Placement (T&P) Officers
* Early-career job seekers

### Problem

Students struggle with interview preparation due to:

* Lack of personalized feedback
* Interview anxiety
* Fragmented learning resources
* Expensive coaching alternatives

### Solution

AI Interview Coach offers:

* AI-powered mock interviews
* Real-time answer evaluation
* Skill-gap reports
* Personalized improvement plans
* Company-specific interview preparation
