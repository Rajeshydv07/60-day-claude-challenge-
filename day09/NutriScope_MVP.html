Nutrition Education
Understanding the science behind your food
🍞
Carbohydrates
Primary energy source. 4 kcal/g. Choose complex carbs (oats, whole grains, legumes) over refined. Fiber-rich carbs support gut health and blood sugar stability. Target: 45–65% of total calories.
🥩
Protein
Builds and repairs tissues. 4 kcal/g. Essential amino acids come from complete proteins (eggs, meat, dairy, soy). Indian vegetarians should combine dal+rice for complete amino acids. Target: 0.8–2g/kg body weight.
🥑
Fat
Essential for hormones, vitamins absorption, and brain. 9 kcal/g. Prioritize unsaturated fats (nuts, seeds, olive oil). Limit saturated and avoid trans fats. Target: 20–35% of total calories.
🌾
Dietary Fiber
Insoluble fiber aids digestion; soluble fiber lowers cholesterol and blood sugar. Found in whole grains, legumes, vegetables, fruits. Target: 25–38g/day. Most Indians are fiber-deficient.
💧
Hydration
Water is the most essential nutrient. Adequate hydration (8–10 glasses/day) supports metabolism, cognition, and kidney health. Hot climates like India increase requirements. NutriScope does not track water — set daily reminders.
