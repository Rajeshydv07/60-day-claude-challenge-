Wow Insights
Alphabet knows more than you think
With Search, YouTube, Pay, and Photos — Alphabet likely holds an estimated profile covering your interests, income proxies, purchases, face-linked photos, and physical movement patterns. All four services can cross-reference your identity.
TikTok is the highest cross-border risk
ByteDance (China-based parent) is subject to different data governance laws. TikTok has faced regulatory scrutiny in the US, EU, and India (banned in India since 2020 — if you're using it, this is a significant legal and privacy risk).
Meta links Instagram + WhatsApp silently
Using both Instagram and WhatsApp under the same Meta umbrella means your messaging patterns and social content behaviour are potentially cross-referenced — even if you treat them as separate apps.
Google Photos is a biometric vault
Face grouping in Google Photos processes facial geometry. Depending on your region's laws and account settings, this may constitute biometric data processing — one of the most sensitive data categories globally.
Gaming apps collect more than scores
PUBG Mobile (Krafton) and Roblox both request device permissions, microphone access, and contact data on mobile. In-app purchase history builds a spending behaviour profile over time.
Final Verdict
Your digital shadow is large — and mostly in corporate hands
With 15 services across 7 parent companies, your data is fragmented yet cross-referenceable. Alphabet alone has an estimated 4-service view of your life. The combination of social, financial, and search data creates a detailed inferred profile that ad networks, data brokers, and potentially bad actors can access or purchase.
