# 🚀 Day 35 – Combined Customer Intelligence Analytics Platform

> **ABTalks × Claude AI – 60 Days Challenge**
> Phase: Customer Intelligence Platform Integration

## 📌 Overview

Day 35 focused on integrating multiple Machine Learning and Analytics modules into a single Customer Intelligence Platform.

Instead of working with isolated models, I combined customer segmentation, recommendation systems, anomaly detection, forecasting, and executive dashboards into one unified analytics workflow.

This represents how modern organizations build end-to-end AI-powered business intelligence systems.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Integrate previously developed analytics modules
- Build a unified customer intelligence workflow
- Connect ML outputs into one dashboard
- Improve business decision-making
- Design an executive-friendly analytics platform
