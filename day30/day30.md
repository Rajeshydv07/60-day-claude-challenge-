# 🚚 Supply Chain Builder

An interactive **Supply Chain Strategy Simulator** that helps users make real-world supply chain decisions and instantly see their impact on business performance. Each choice updates key business metrics, allowing users to build an optimized and resilient supply chain.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Supply Chain Builder is a decision-based simulation where users act as a **Supply Chain Manager**. The application guides users through six strategic decisions, including supplier selection, factory location, warehouse strategy, transportation method, and inventory planning.

Every decision dynamically updates important business KPIs, allowing users to understand the trade-offs involved in supply chain optimization.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 💰 Cost
- ⚡ Delivery Speed
- ⚠️ Risk
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction
- 🌱 Sustainability

After completing all decisions, users receive a **Final Optimization Dashboard** with an overall performance score, strengths, weaknesses, and actionable business insights.

## 🧩 Decision Workflow

### 1️⃣ Supplier Strategy
Choose between different supplier models that balance cost, resilience, and reliability.

### 2️⃣ Factory Location
Select the optimal manufacturing location while considering production cost, delivery speed, and operational risk.

### 3️⃣ Warehouse Strategy
Optimize inventory distribution using centralized or regional warehouse networks.

### 4️⃣ Transportation Method
Choose logistics options such as Air, Sea, Rail, or Truck based on delivery priorities and sustainability.

### 5️⃣ Inventory Strategy
Implement an inventory model that balances stock availability, customer demand, and operational efficiency.

### 6️⃣ Final Optimization Dashboard
Analyze your complete supply chain performance through business metrics, recommendations, and an overall optimization score.
